Hold L2 or R2 and drag upward to vary its pressure. Your connected gamepad can also drive the display. Auto follows the controls you use: triggers show the rear, shoulder buttons show the top, and face buttons show the front. Pressed buttons glow soft yellow; triggers also show their pressure. Enable touchpad to follow one or two fingers on the physical pad without clicking, or drag the on-screen pad to see the circle. No vibration is added. Dragging the shell or selecting Front, Angle, or Back pauses Auto; choose Auto to resume. To feel real trigger resistance, connect a DualSense and choose Enable trigger effects. Select your controller in the browser prompt, then squeeze L2 or R2. Pistol has a crisp break; Shotgun has a heavier, longer pull. Let go to rearm either one. LMG gives strong repeated pulses while held down; SMG gives faster, lighter pulses. Resistance applies a steady force. Desktop Chrome or Edge is required; try a USB data cable if Bluetooth is unavailable. Off, Reset, and leaving the tab release the effect. Microphone control remains a visual simulation.
